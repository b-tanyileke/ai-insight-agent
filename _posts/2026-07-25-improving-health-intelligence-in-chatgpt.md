## What it is
OpenAI has updated ChatGPT with GPT-5.5 Instant, enhancing its health and wellness responses through stronger reasoning, better context, and clearer communication, all informed by physician evaluations.

## Business use case
Businesses in healthcare, wellness, or related customer service can leverage ChatGPT for more reliable and accurate health and wellness information. This could support patient education, answer common queries, or assist in content creation, improving user trust and potentially reducing human workload for routine information provision.

## Estimated value
The value is qualitative, stemming from increased trust and reliability in AI-generated health information. This could lead to improved user engagement, reduced operational costs for handling routine health inquiries, and enhanced brand reputation for businesses utilizing it, though specific monetary value is uncertain without implementation details.

## Implementation
Businesses already using or planning to integrate ChatGPT would likely benefit from these improvements by updating to GPT-5.5 Instant. Implementation would involve leveraging the updated model through existing ChatGPT interfaces or APIs, potentially requiring refinement of prompts for specific health and wellness applications.
