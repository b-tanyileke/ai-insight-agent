## What it is
Anthropic updated Claude Fable 5's biology safeguards, reducing false-positive classifier triggers by approximately 85%. This allows the system to process everyday health, educational, and clinical support queries directly without falling back to less capable models.

## Business use case
Healthcare professionals and clinical support personnel can use Fable 5 for interpreting lab results, analyzing symptoms, and assisting with clinical tasks with far fewer fallback interruptions.

## Estimated value
Qualitative value through improved user experience and higher model capability for clinical tasks, though exact monetary impact is uncertain and depends on organizational adoption.

## Implementation
Requires accessing Claude Fable 5 through standard product surfaces; the 85% fallback reduction is applied automatically, though dual-use tasks like virology and molecular design remain blocked.
