## What it is
DXC Technology, a major IT services company, is forming a multi-year global alliance with Anthropic to integrate Claude AI into the critical systems of banks, airlines, and other regulated industries. DXC will train thousands of engineers to deploy Claude within these complex, high-security environments.

## Business use case
This alliance directly impacts IT operations and modernization within highly regulated sectors. Specifically, it enables faster and more accurate legacy code modernization, enhanced cybersecurity operations through AI subagents, and improved application maintenance and management by embedding Claude into existing workflows.

## Estimated value
The text suggests a significant acceleration in software development, with DXC estimating Claude sped up code generation by a factor of 10 for its OASIS platform. For customers, this could translate to reduced IT modernization costs and faster deployment of new capabilities, though specific financial figures are not provided.

## Implementation
DXC will leverage its existing workforce, training tens of thousands of engineers through Anthropic Academy and its own specialized curriculum. This requires significant investment in training and certification, as well as the integration of Claude into DXC's existing managed services and customer systems.
