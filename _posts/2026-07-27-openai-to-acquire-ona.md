## What it is
OpenAI plans to acquire Ona to integrate secure, persistent cloud environments into Codex. This acquisition aims to enable long-running AI agents that can execute across enterprise workflows.

## Business use case
Software engineering and enterprise process automation teams can leverage persistent execution environments for AI agents to complete complex, multi-step, long-running tasks.

## Estimated value
Financial value is uncertain, but qualitatively it enables continuous background agent execution, reducing manual intervention in long-running enterprise processes.

## Implementation
Prerequisites include using OpenAI Codex and enterprise workflows, with effort depending on OpenAI's timeline for integrating Ona's cloud environments into Codex.
