## What it is
Anthropic has released a native connector in claude.ai that allows users to directly query the Anthropic Economic Index through conversation. The dataset tracks how AI is used across various occupations, tasks, locations, and automation patterns over time.

## Business use case
Workforce planning, HR, and strategy teams can query the tool to evaluate how specific occupations and tasks in their industry are being supplemented or automated by AI. This assists leaders in benchmarking role evolution and identifying task-level automation trends.

## Estimated value
Direct monetary value is uncertain as financial return depends on how effectively organizations apply the insights to talent and operations strategies.

## Implementation
Minimal effort required; users simply open the connectors menu in claude.ai and enable the Anthropic Economic Index connector with no software installation required.
