## What it is
A partnership between Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and Anthropic to deploy Anthropic's Claude AI model within regulated industries. TCS will integrate Claude into its own operations and develop client-facing products powered by Claude.

## Business use case
Financial services, healthcare, and public sector organizations can leverage this partnership to implement Claude for tasks requiring high accuracy and auditability, such as claims processing and lending advisory, with the support of TCS's compliance and integration expertise.

## Estimated value
The value is framed qualitatively as enabling faster time-to-production for AI solutions, particularly in trust-sensitive sectors. Specific financial figures are not provided, but the scale of TCS's employee base (50,000) and client reach suggests a significant potential impact.

## Implementation
Requires TCS to build a dedicated practice with consultants, engineers, and industry specialists. It also involves packaging Claude into industry-specific offerings and integrating it with existing client systems. TCS will also provide training and certification for Claude.
