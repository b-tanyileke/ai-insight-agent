## What it is
Anthropic and Cognizant expanded their partnership, designating Cognizant a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network. Cognizant is embedding Claude into its internal engineering and IT platforms, including Flowsource and Neuro, while scaling a workforce with over 30,000 Claude-trained associates.

## Business use case
This affects enterprise teams in manufacturing, life sciences, and insurance by integrating Claude into daily workflows via Cognizant's platforms. Key applications include software engineering with Claude Code, biopharmaceutical contract intelligence, and automated risk evaluation for insurance underwriters.

## Estimated value
Reported deployment results include cutting contract review time by up to 40% with over 88% extraction accuracy, and saving insurance underwriters roughly 8 hours per week by reducing research time from hours to minutes.

## Implementation
Requires engaging Cognizant to deploy enterprise solutions, utilizing their Flowsource and Neuro platforms along with their Claude-certified engineering teams.
