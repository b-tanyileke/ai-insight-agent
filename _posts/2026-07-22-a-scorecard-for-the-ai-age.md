## What it is
A practical AI scorecard introduced by OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar to help organizations evaluate the return on investment of AI initiatives.

## Business use case
CFOs, technology executives, and operations leaders can apply this framework to measure AI deployments across useful work, cost per successful task, dependability, and return on compute.

## Estimated value
Monetary value is uncertain and dependent on the organization, but the framework helps clarify unit economics and resource efficiency for AI projects.

## Implementation
Requires minimal technical effort to adopt conceptually, but organizations must establish baseline tracking for task success rates, compute expenses, dependability, and work output.
