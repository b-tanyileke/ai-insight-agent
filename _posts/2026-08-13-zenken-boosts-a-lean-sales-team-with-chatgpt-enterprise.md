## What it is
Zenken executed a company-wide deployment of ChatGPT Enterprise focused on powering its sales operations. The rollout targeted reducing preparation time and driving higher proposal success rates.

## Business use case
Business development and proposal management teams can leverage ChatGPT Enterprise to accelerate proposal preparation and client pitch creation. This directly supports the firm's goal to increase proposal productivity using an already approved vendor (OpenAI).

## Estimated value
Qualitative value includes reduced proposal preparation time, enhanced sales team performance, and higher proposal success rates, though specific numerical impact figures are not provided.

## Implementation
Requires procuring ChatGPT Enterprise licenses, configuring enterprise security controls to protect confidential proposal data, and training business development staff on proposal prep workflows.
