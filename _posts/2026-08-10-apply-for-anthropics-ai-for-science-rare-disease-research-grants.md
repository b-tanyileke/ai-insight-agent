## What it is
Anthropic launched a targeted rare disease grant initiative within its AI for Science program, offering accepted applicants up to $50,000 in Claude credits over six months across basic research and biotech clinical development tracks.

## Business use case
Early-stage biotechs and clinical research teams can use Claude via API to analyze patient registries, synthesize scientific literature, extract insights from small datasets, and discover shared disease mechanisms.

## Estimated value
Provides up to $50,000 in API credit subsidies per grantee over six months, with broader value depending on gains in research speed and target discovery.

## Implementation
Requires submitting a grant application to Anthropic's program, alongside existing clinical data science expertise to integrate Claude API endpoints or frameworks like Monarch's DisMech library into research pipelines.
